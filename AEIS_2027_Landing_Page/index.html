ABOUT THE SUMMIT

Education is changing.
Let's shape what comes next.

The Africa Education & Innovation Summit (AEIS 2027) is a platform for educators, learners, institutions, innovators and decision-makers to explore practical solutions for the future of education.

Through conversations, collaboration and innovation-focused activities, the summit will create space to share ideas, discover opportunities and help shape a more inclusive, technology-enabled education ecosystem across Africa.
